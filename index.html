Where the city ends in stone and sea.

Ondine sits on a granite ledge above Bantry Bay. Lion’s Head rises behind it, Table Mountain holds the skyline, and to the west there is only the Atlantic.

33.92° S · 18.38° E — 40 m above the tide line

A pool that runs to the horizon.

An eighteen-metre infinity edge, cantilevered over the rocks. Bleached-oak terraces step out from every room, so mornings begin outside and evenings end there.

Curved glass. No threshold.

The façade follows the cliff in one sweep of board-formed concrete and floor-to-ceiling glass. The doors slide away into the walls, and terrace and living room become a single floor.

Atelier Ndlovu Maas · Completed 2024

A room the height of two.

The living room rises through both floors and fills with western light. Travertine underfoot, white oak in the joinery, linen at the glass: materials chosen to age quietly.
